Norsu Fit privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Norsu Fit is a training and nutrition coach for iPhone. It is built so that your data stays with you. This page says what the app keeps, where it goes, and what it never does.

The short version

  • Your data lives on your iPhone. We run no server that stores it, and we have no account system.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The app contains no advertising or analytics code and never sells or shares your data.
  • The AI you choose is the only place your words go, and only when you use the companions.

What the app keeps on your phone

What you tell it and log in it: your profile and goals, meals, water, training, walks, readings such as weight, medicines if you add them, and your conversations with the companions. It is stored in the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can export it from the app at any time.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads health data such as weight, measurements, blood pressure, glucose, sleep, steps, activity and workouts, and saves your meals, water, weight and workouts to Apple Health. You choose exactly which types in the Health permission screen and can change it in Settings. Health data is never used for advertising and never sold.

The AI companions

The companions need an AI model to answer. You choose how in the app:

  • Templates only: nothing leaves your phone.
  • Your own API key: when you send a message or log by voice or photo, the relevant text, your recent logs and goals needed for the answer, and any photo you attach are sent to the provider you picked (for example OpenRouter, Groq or Anthropic) under that provider's own privacy terms. Your key is kept in the iPhone Keychain.
  • A paired computer of your own: the same information goes, encrypted over HTTPS, to a computer you set up and pair yourself, which passes it to the AI service you run there.

Voice, camera, photos and location

  • Microphone and speech: only when you tap the microphone. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone by Apple, or on your own paired computer if you set one up. Recordings are not kept.
  • Camera: only when you scan a barcode, photograph a meal or a medicine box, or scan a pairing code.
  • Barcodes: a scanned barcode number is looked up in the public Open Food Facts database. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Location: to draw the route of a walk you start, and to find your city for prayer times. It stays on your phone.
  • Photos: the app only adds images to your library when you ask it to save one.

Children

Norsu Fit is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
